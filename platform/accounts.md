# Accounts, usernames & billing

## Account
Sign up at [world.mercury-hq.com/account](https://world.mercury-hq.com/account) — username + password, no email required (email accounts work too: use your email as the username at sign-in, then claim a username).

Your username is your world address segment: `world.mercury-hq.com/<username>/world1`.

One login covers every MERCURY app on mercury-hq.com (a single secure session cookie on the domain — see the [Privacy Policy](https://world.mercury-hq.com/legal/privacy)).
